# Governance

## Maintainer

EGX_Terminal is currently maintained by Maxime Erard under the public GitHub
identity [@ElGrandeXu](https://github.com/ElGrandeXu). Governance is
maintainer-led. There is no committee or organization behind the project.

## Decision process

Ordinary changes are reviewed through pull requests. Structural changes are
recorded in Architecture Decision Records (ADRs), and scope changes require
evidence proportionate to their cost. Negative results are retained. An
experiment is never promoted automatically into the active repository root.

## Project boundaries

The project distinguishes documented principles, voluntarily selected
protocols, archived experiments, and active capabilities. Complex future
capabilities must be explicit and opt-in. Hidden instruction or payload
injection is outside the project boundary.

## Maintainer authority

The maintainer may reject out-of-scope changes, request additional evidence,
and protect experimental archives and provenance. Submission creates no promise
of merge, response time, or delivery date.

## Licensing

Each file is governed by the license assigned to its location in `REUSE.toml`.
Contributions are accepted under the license already applicable there. The
project currently uses neither a Contributor License Agreement nor a Developer
Certificate of Origin. Contributors must hold the rights needed to submit their
work and its dependencies.

## Evolution

Governance may evolve if the maintainer or contributor population warrants it.
Any material governance change will be documented by ADR.

The Code of Conduct decision is `DEFERRED_UNTIL_ENFORCEABLE`: the project will
revisit it when a private reporting channel, independent of the maintainer's
personal address, can protect reporters and support real enforcement. This is a
practical deferral, not opposition to codes of conduct.
